## Caveats

Capped convergence figures quoted elsewhere (61% / 88% / 98%) are from the published capped runs; the per-set capped non-convergence here is an unweighted cell mean. One configuration, one mask seed.

Convergence fractions (P72, P143, and the full-N table above) cover every scenario WLS ran (up to 100 per grid, 30 on whole grids), while the win counts use the 30/10 comparator prefix. The audit measured the informed-uncapped fractions on the prefix: locked 79.6% (vs 82.5% full N), seen pieces 89.0%, unseen pieces 70.6%, whole grids 50.6% (audit F3, round 1). The draft sentences already say 'up to 100 scenarios per grid', so the wording stands; the locked-15 prefix figure is 80%, not 83%, if a like-for-like number is wanted. GENCO and interpolators exist only on the prefix.
