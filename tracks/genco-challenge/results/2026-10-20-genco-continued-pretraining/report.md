# B: continued pretraining of the released GENCO on corrected vs original labels (2026-10-19/20)

**UNAUDITED.** Protocol `protocol.json` (20:25, written before any run of this design). Two arms on the local RTX 3090s
(`experiments/b-continue/`):
- Both start from the released pretraining checkpoint, with LR 3e-5 constant and 20 epochs (about 5,000 updates).
- They use the same 7 training subgrids and are identical except for the labels: `fixed` has corrected labels,
  `published` the original ones.

Scoring:
- `run_scoring.sh`: zero-shot on the 15 held-out grids at step 500 and at the final state, plus collapse
  reproduction under the pretraining mask.
- `pool.py` -> `pooled.md` (7 training subgrids, mask seeds 1000/1001/1002).
- Preservation ratios -> `preservation.json`.

## Bottom line

1. **Corrected labels unlearn the collapse only partly.**
   - The share of hidden collapsed PQ buses that GENCO still predicts below 0.5 pu falls from 0.970 to 0.979
     (released, by mask seed) to 0.834 to 0.846 (corrected labels, final).
   - The same updates on the original labels end at 0.935 to 0.941.
   - The labels therefore account for about 10 percentage points of unlearning, but about 84 percent of the
     collapse remains after about 5,000 updates.
2. **Preservation FAILS by the pre-registered rule, so no "GENCO-corrected" checkpoint is published.** The result is
   reported as unlearning with a cost.

   | test (corrected arm, final state) | measured | margin | result |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | held-out 15: median ratio to released, branch-angle error | 1.16 | ≤ 1.10 | FAIL |
   | held-out 15: median ratio to released, \|V\| error | 1.35 | ≤ 1.10 | FAIL |
   | normal-scenario hidden \|V\| MAE on the training subgrids, ratio to released | 1.46 | ≤ 1.5 | pass |
   | healthy low-voltage bias (repair-v3 F4 rows) | not run | ±0.05 pu | not run |

3. **The original labels do more damage than the corrected ones.**

   | measure | corrected arm | original-label arm |
   |---|---|---|
   | held-out branch-angle ratio to released | 1.16 | 1.44 |
   | held-out \|V\| ratio to released | 1.35 | 2.31 |
   | normal \|V\| MAE ratio to released | 1.46 | 2.08 |
   | grids where branch-angle error beats released (of 15) | 3 | 0 |

4. **Damage is already visible at step 500.**
   - Held-out branch-angle ratio: 1.11 (corrected) and 1.12 (original).
   - Held-out \|V\| ratio: 1.39 and 1.47.
   - Continuing to pretrain on 7 narrow subgrids erodes transfer in both arms. That is the risk control arm D of the
     transfer experiment (`../2026-10-20-genco-transfer/`) measures.

## Notes

- **Step 0** is the released weights in both arms. The two `state_step0.pt` files are byte-identical (md5
  eff2b2b6...), so the complete `published_step0` collapse run stands for both. `fixed_step0.jsonl` is incomplete
  (7 of 9 grids) after an interrupted run.
- **Full unlearning curve** (all saved steps scored, `pooled.md`; audit R1-F9, 2026-10-20 16:40). On the corrected
  labels, reproduction stays at 0.94-0.96 through step 250, falls to 0.92 by step 500 and 0.91 by step 1000, and
  reaches 0.83-0.85 only at the final state (~5,000 updates). On the original labels it stays at 0.91-0.97
  throughout. Normal-scenario |V| MAE jumps from 0.0014 to ~0.002 within the first 25 updates in both arms.
- `preservation.json` is generated by `preservation.py` (audit R1-F8; regenerated byte-identical).
